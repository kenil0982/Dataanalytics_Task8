# Dataanalytics_Task8
📊 Adidas US Sales Analysis
This project presents a clean and insightful analysis of Adidas US sales data. It focuses on generating short, actionable insights from sales figures across regions, products, and retail methods.

📁 Dataset
Source: Internal Adidas US Sales data

File: Adidas US Sales Cleaned Datasets.xlsx

Sheet: Data Sales Adidas

Records: 9,648 entries

Columns Include:

Retailer, Product, Region, State, City

Price per Unit, Units Sold, Total Sales

Operating Profit, Operating Margin

Sales Method, Invoice Date

📌 Key Short Insights
West region had the highest total sales overall.

South region had the highest sales in Q3.

The top-selling product category was Men's Street Footwear.

New York had the highest sales among all states.

In-store purchases generated more revenue than Online purchases.

Sales peaked in month 7 (July).

Additional insights (e.g., most profitable product, top-selling retailer, etc.) are included in the extended analysis.
